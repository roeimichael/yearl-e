About yearl-e

Each day, a random historical year is rolled (deterministic — same year for everyone). You click the globe where you'd want to live. Your score is based on the actual living conditions of that region in that year.

The 5 factors

  • Safety — active wars/rebellions that year from the Brecke Conflict Catalog (to 1999) and UCDP/PRIO (2000+). Each conflict touching a region costs points.
  • Health — life expectancy (Our World in Data): the region's country where known, else its continental aggregate; adjusted for war and wealth.
  • Economy — Maddison Project 2023 GDP/capita where available, else a modeled regional baseline; ranked into a percentile across the year.
  • Governance — V-Dem v15 Electoral Democracy Index from 1789; the State Antiquity Index (state-continuity) as a proxy before that.
  • Religious tolerance — V-Dem's measured freedom-of-religion index from 1789; before that, a modeled regional baseline lowered by recorded persecution (Leeson-Russ witch-trial database, Europe 1300–1850).

Overall score is a weighted blend of the five factors — and the weights shift with the year: a violent year counts peace most, an age of persecution counts tolerance most. The same weights apply to every region that year, and the reveal shows which factor mattered.

Data sources

  • Maddison Project Database 2023 — GDP per capita, 1 AD–2022
  • Brecke Conflict Catalog — global wars 1400–1999
  • UCDP/PRIO Armed Conflict Dataset — armed conflicts 1946–2024
  • V-Dem v15 — Electoral Democracy Index 1789–present
  • State Antiquity Index — pre-1789 state-continuity proxy
  • Our World in Data — life expectancy (Riley; Zijdeman; UN)
  • Leeson-Russ Witch Trials — recorded persecution, Europe 1300–1850
  • Cliopatria (Seshat) — period-accurate political borders, 3400 BCE–2024 CE (CC-BY 4.0)
  • Natural Earth — modern country shapes (used to map polities to country data)

Known limitations

  • Regions are real historical polities from Cliopatria, with borders sampled every ~25 years — so the map shifts as the centuries pass.
  • Each region's economy/governance/health are read from the modern country it overlaps, so they're proxies, not exact figures. The reveal labels each factor real vs modeled.
  • Pre-1789 governance uses a state-continuity proxy (it rewards stable, established states); religious tolerance outside Europe is a modeled regional baseline.
  • Covers 1500–2026 across two eras. Earlier eras (back to 1000 BCE) are coming next.
